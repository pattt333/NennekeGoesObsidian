# Initiative

Initiative determines the order in which characters and creatures act during combat.

## Rolling Initiative

At the start of combat, each participant rolls initiative:

1. Roll a d20
2. Add your Dexterity modifier
3. Add any bonuses from abilities, feats, or magic items

## Turn Order

Combat proceeds in order from highest to lowest initiative:

- **Highest roll** acts first
- **Ties** are resolved by comparing Dexterity scores (higher Dexterity goes first)
- If still tied, roll off or decide amongst yourselves

## Surprise

If one side is unaware of the other at the start of combat:

- Surprised creatures cannot take actions or reactions during the first round
- They still roll initiative but cannot act until their surprise wears off

## Delaying and Readying

- **Delay**: You may choose to act later in the initiative order
- **Ready**: Prepare an action that triggers under specific conditions
